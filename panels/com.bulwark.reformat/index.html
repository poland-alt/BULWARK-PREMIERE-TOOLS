How this works
Open the vertical clip. Click a window in the preview (or its name) to mark it, then click a picture to fill it. Drag a picture in the preview to move it. "Add image file" puts in any picture from this Mac, or drop an image onto the preview. "Use playhead frame" also works inside any 16x9 sequence, such as a person's camera nest. Double-click a picture option to jump the playhead there. "Export these pictures" saves every option as a full-size PNG; the arrow in a picture's corner saves just that one. Cmd+Z undoes the last change while this tab is showing.

"Change folder…" picks where thumbnails and exported pictures are saved.
