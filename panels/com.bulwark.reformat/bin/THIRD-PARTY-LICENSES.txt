Bulwark Premiere Tools includes a transcriber (bin/ptranscribe) built from these open-source projects.

FluidAudio (https://github.com/FluidInference/FluidAudio), Copyright Fluid Inference, licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). It bundles NemoTextProcessing (text-processing-rs, Fluid Inference).

Speech model, downloaded on first use: NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2 (Copyright NVIDIA, CC-BY-4.0, https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2), converted for Core ML by Fluid Inference.
