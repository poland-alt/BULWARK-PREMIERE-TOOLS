Bulwark Clips & Captions

1. Find clips in a full episode

Open the finished 16x9 episode sequence. The panel transcribes it on this Mac, asks Claude for the best 1:30 to 2:30 moments, and cuts the ones you tick into their own sequences in the bin "SOCIAL CLIPS 16x9".

2. Caption a vertical clip

Open the 9x16 clip. Captions go on a new top track: Special Gothic Condensed SemiBold, white with a black stroke, the word being spoken in gold.

Claude API key

Needed to find clips and to correct words. Paste a Bulwark Anthropic API key once; it is saved on this Mac only. Transcript text is sent to Anthropic; audio and video never leave the Mac.

Caption outline

Change the stroke on captions already in the sequence.
Works on any Motion Graphics Template clip that has an "Outline Width" control (CaptionX Word Fill and the Bulwark template). Other clips are left alone.
